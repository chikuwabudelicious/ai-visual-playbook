# 画風の頭文（そのまま貼る）

プロンプトの先頭に置く「画風」の英文。実際に何百枚も使って品位が安定したもの。
`<…>` は差し替える。色は**5色以内に固定**する（色を足すと途端に安っぽくなる）。

## 色の組（パレット）と意味づけ

色は「意味」に結びつけて使う。図ごとに色を発明しない。

| 組 | 色 | 合う題材 |
|---|---|---|
| 森と琥珀 | deep forest green #1F5E2E, fresh green #2E7D32, earth brown #5A3D2B, warm amber #E8A33D, soft gray #5B5F58 | 環境・農業・地域・食 |
| 紺とティール | deep navy #16263A, teal #1F8A7A, light teal #CFE7E2, amber #F2A33A, coral #E2674A | 技術・インフラ・行政 |
| 藍と金 | deep indigo #16283C, gold #C9A227, emerald #17817A, terracotta #B4522E, warm gray #6E7671 | 格式の高い提案・式典 |
| 深緑とサフラン | dark teal #0D2F2A, saffron #F4A300, leaf green #A1C181, coral #E56B2F, slate blue #426B77 | データ・調査・報告 |

意味の例：緑＝解決・良い状態、琥珀＝価値・強調、テラコッタ／コーラル＝課題・排出・警告（**減らしたいものだけに使う**）、ティール＝水、茶＝土・原料。

---

## S1 フラットな図解（データ・説明資料の標準）

```
Modern premium FLAT-DESIGN infographic for <a public-sector briefing on TOPIC>. Widescreen 16:9 canvas,
off-white background #F4F6F1 filling the whole canvas, no border frame. Strict color system only:
deep forest green #1F5E2E, fresh green #2E7D32, earth brown #5A3D2B, warm amber #E8A33D,
soft neutral gray #5B5F58, plus pale tints of these. Thin elegant two-tone line-art icons and small
illustrated scenes with consistent stroke weight, amber numbered badges with white numerals,
soft subtle shadows. The diagram must FILL the canvas: large elements, large legible labels,
no big empty areas, but keep a clean 4% margin so nothing touches the edges. Sophisticated, calm,
trustworthy editorial look — NOT cartoonish, no emoji, no clip-art, no photos. No watermark, no logo,
no overall title at the top (the slide has its own title).
```

背景色をスライドの地の色と同じにすると、貼ったときに継ぎ目が出ない。

## S2 アイソメトリックの箱庭

```
Modern premium ISOMETRIC infographic illustration (clean 30-degree isometric 3D blocks and miniature
scenes), editorial and calm, for <AUDIENCE>. Widescreen 16:9 canvas, warm white background #FAF7F2
filling the whole canvas, no border frame. Strict palette only: deep navy #16263A, teal #1F8A7A,
light teal #CFE7E2, amber #F2A33A, coral #E2674A (only for problems), warm gray #8C877E,
light sand #ECE6DA. Soft long shadows, crisp edges, gentle gradients, high detail miniatures.
The illustration must FILL the canvas with large elements and large legible labels, but keep a clean
4% margin. Labels sit on small white rounded tags or directly on the background in navy.
NOT cartoonish, no emoji, no photos, no watermark, no logo, no overall title at the top.
```

## S3 文字の無い挿絵（絵本調の線画＋淡彩）

```
Portrait editorial illustration for a business briefing slide. Hand-drawn fine ink outlines with soft,
muted flat colour washes, in the style of a calm technical storybook: palette of deep forest green,
sage green, warm cream (#F6F2E7), warm brown and ochre accents, small touches of muted blue.
One clear main scene, uncluttered, gently vignetted so the edges fade into a plain warm cream background.
ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT ANYWHERE: no letters, no words, no numbers, no labels, no captions, no writing on
signs, papers, screens, sacks or vehicles, no logos, no brand names, no flags. No famous landmarks,
no identifiable city skyline. People are generic and respectful.
Setting: <a generic temperate countryside with broadleaf trees and low green hills / a generic
warm-climate town with a few palm trees and low houses with metal roofs>, not identifiable as any
specific country.
```

「文字なし」は思いつく限り列挙する（袋・画面・車体の字まで）。1つでも抜けるとそこに字が出る。

## S4 横長4枚組のカード（3:1）

```
A wide horizontal infographic strip, 3:1, on a warm cream background (#F7F1E6). Four rounded white
cards in one row, left to right, joined by small orange chevrons. Each card: a detailed realistic
painted illustration on top (soft natural light, polished semi-realistic style), a round orange badge
with the number 1-4 at the top-left corner, and a dark green rounded label bar at the bottom with
WHITE bold text in a clean sans. The ONLY text in the whole image is the four labels, exactly as given,
and the badge numbers. No other words, no logos, no brand names, no flags, no captions, no watermarks.
Card 1: <scene> — label '<…>'. Card 2: … Card 3: … Card 4: …
```

## S5 その国らしい意匠（相手国向けの提案）

```
<COUNTRY> design sensibility for a proposal to <RECIPIENT>. Warm off-white handmade-paper background
<#FAF6EC>. Strict color system only: <5 colors with hex>. Thin <gold> hairline rules; a restrained
<traditional ornament> motif used only as a faint corner ornament; very low in the background a pale
silhouette band of <a characteristic natural landscape, not a landmark>. Modern premium FLAT-DESIGN
infographic. No outer border frame, no drop shadows. Elegant two-tone line-art icons of consistent
stroke weight, drawn LARGE and confident. Numbered badges are small filled circles with white numerals.
Generous white space, calm, dignified. NOT cartoonish, NOT emoji, no clip-art, no photographs, no 3D,
no religious figures, no deities, no human faces. No watermark, no logo.
```

- 伝統模様は「隅の薄い飾りだけ」に抑える。全面に使うと土産物のようになる。
- 宗教的な像・国旗・有名な建物は入れない（相手国で不快に受け取られうる）。

## S6 スライド下端の帯（横長の風景・水彩）

```
Ultra-wide distant panorama that runs continuously across the whole width, left edge to right edge,
with no border and no frame. It is a LOW, FLAT strip of scenery seen from far away: every element
(trees, hills, houses, people, vehicles) fits inside a thin horizontal strip from 72% to 100% of the
image height, and the tallest thing is no taller than 16% of the image height. Elements are small and
evenly spread along the whole width. Everything above 72% of the height is empty, perfectly flat,
plain background color <#F7F6EC> with no texture, no gradient, no clouds. The ground reaches the very
bottom edge of the image. The place is neutral and not identifiable as any particular country or
region (no flags, no landmarks, no churches or steeples, no distinctive national architecture).
Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no signs, no logos, no watermarks.
Soft hand-drawn picture-book illustration: thin dark-green ink outlines with gentle watercolor washes,
palette of fresh greens, warm cream, a touch of ochre and soft brown, calm and friendly.
Scene: <a small market town / farmland with a country road / a quiet river valley with a footbridge>.
```

- 3:1 で描かせ、下の 1/4 だけを切り出して 12:1 の帯にする（スライド側の道具で切る）。
- 上部を「平らな単色」と言い切るのがコツ。雲や空のグラデーションが入ると切り口が目立つ。
- 「特定の国に見せない」は**具体的に禁止を並べる**（教会の尖塔、瓦屋根、桜…）。抽象的に言うだけだと、モデルの癖の国の家並みが出る。

## S7 帯（リソグラフ調）

S6 の最後の1文を次に替える：

```
Risograph print style: flat shapes with a subtle grain, limited palette of dark teal ink (#0D2F2A),
saffron (#F4A300), soft leaf green (#A1C181), coral and pale sky blue, lively and graphic.
```

ほかに合った例：「銅版画風の細線（fine etching hatching）」「切り絵（paper cut-out layers）」「夕暮れのシルエット（暗い面用）」。

## S8 動画の部品シート（12個を1枚に）

```
Flat vector illustration set, friendly, clean and modern, like a premium explainer video. Rounded
shapes, consistent medium outline in dark green-gray #2B3A31, soft flat shading. Palette: <5 colors>,
plus natural colors where needed (food, metal, glass). Exactly 12 separate items arranged in a neat
grid of 4 columns by 3 rows on a plain PURE WHITE background (#FFFFFF), each item isolated and centered
in its own cell with generous empty white space around it; items must not touch or overlap.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no frames or borders,
no ground shadows connecting items.
Items (row by row): 1 <…>, 2 <…>, … 12 <…>.
```

1枚で12個作り、格子で切り分けると画風が完全にそろう（1個ずつ作ると毎回絵柄が揺れる）。

## S9 透過アイコン（線画）

```
Flat vector icon, minimal, bold simple shapes, thick clean outlines, limited palette of forest green,
moss green, amber and warm brown, centered, generous margin, no gradient mesh, no text, no letters,
no shadow on the ground. Subject: <…>.
```

`background="transparent"` で生成する。アイコンは濃い色の面の上に置かない（沈む）。

## S10 表紙・結びの写真風（文字なし・言語共通）

```
Cinematic editorial photograph, muted natural tones, slightly desaturated, shallow depth of field,
documentary feel. No text, no people's faces in focus, no logos, no watermark. 16:9.
<A clean modern facility interior with soft daylight from high windows … / Healthy dark soil with a
young seedling, warm golden hour light, farmland softly blurred behind …>
```

文字の無い写真は1枚を全言語で使い回せる（生成費が言語数分の1になる）。上に文字を載せるときは暗幕（グラデーションの帯）を敷く。

## S11 記録写真風（現場の雰囲気）

```
A realistic photo of <scene>, natural ambient lighting, rich textures, authentic documentary photo
aesthetic, shallow depth of field. Background people are small, out of focus and not identifiable.
No text, no signage, no watermark, no logo.
```

生成した写真を「現地の記録」「実績」として使わない。説明のための情景として扱う。

## S12 断面図（技術の説明）

```
A clean cross-section cutaway of <a sealed horizontal cylindrical vessel with an internal mixing
screw>, technical but elegant line-art style on an off-white background, strict palette <…>,
four amber numbered callouts pointing to parts: 1 '<…>', 2 '<…>', 3 '<…>', 4 '<…>'.
Labels in <language>, exactly as given. No other text.
```

部品の名前を番号つきの吹き出しにすると、図の中の文字が少なくて済み、崩れにくい。
